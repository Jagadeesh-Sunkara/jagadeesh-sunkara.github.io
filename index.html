About me

From understanding finance to engineering it.

I started my career as a business analyst, sitting with finance teams to understand how money, contracts and reports really move through a company. That curiosity pulled me into engineering — first building lease-accounting software for global banks and lessors, and today building investment platforms and AI tools in New York.

Along the way I earned a Master's in Data Science (3.97 GPA), led a student product-management club and supported early-stage startups. I care about software that is fast, secure and genuinely easy to use — and I love turning messy, manual processes into something people enjoy.
